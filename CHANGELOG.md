# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

This project follows a Keep a Changelog style and Semantic Versioning principles for release notes.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Home-screen widget "Mood and practice of the day" (Android): five faces save a check-in in one tap and open the journal on it, where a note or factors can be added; a second tap replaces the current morning or evening check-in, as in the journal. "Practice of the day" opens today's practice. The widget shows nothing personal. Checked on the emulator: placing it, a tap with the app running and after its process was killed, the replacement of the slot's check-in; dark theme and English were not checked on the widget.
- Mood check-in reminder "at my usual time" (now the default for new installs): the hour with the most check-ins of the last 30 days, from at least five of them, otherwise 20:00. A reminder no longer comes on a day the person already checked in within three hours before it; a morning check-in does not silence the evening one. A time chosen earlier is kept. Checked on the emulator: the option and the scheduled alarm; a reminder actually firing at a learned hour was not waited for.
- "What helps me" in the menu: built only from the person's own on-device records. It shows practices after which tension drops (the average before-and-after drop over at least two sessions), how each test result changed since the first try, with an offer to retake it after two weeks, journal factors that go with a higher or lower mood (at least three entries, a difference of 0.3 or more, worded as a link, not a cause), and repeated Body check-in patterns. Checked on the emulator in Russian with sparse data (empty hints, a single test); full data, dark theme and English were not checked on a device.

### Fixed
- Journal factor chips: a second tap on a chip («Прогулка», «Сон») added the line again instead of removing it, the chip never showed as selected, and a factor without text after the colon was not counted in the journal analysis or "What helps me". A factor line now matches with or without text after the colon; notes already saved with repeated lines show each factor once, and the next tap on its chip removes all its repeats. The journal factor tests now run in `dotnet test` on Windows as well. Seen on the emulator in Russian (light theme): «Прогулка» then «Сон» select both chips and add one line each, a second tap on each removes its line. Not seen on a device: English, the dark theme, "What helps me" with factor-only entries, and an old note with repeated lines (both are covered by tests).

## [2.008] - 2026-10-18

Clinical texts of this release (trauma work, PTSD tests, Body check-in, courses) are to be checked by a psychologist before it is published: [docs/clinical-review.md](docs/clinical-review.md).

### Removed
- The psychosomatics reference (the "Body" tab): a table in the manner of Louise Hay that tied illnesses to emotions ("an abscess: thoughts of revenge", "adenoids: a child who feels unwanted") and suggested practices by the word searched (knee pain → "Experience modification"). The claims have no scientific basis and can do harm: people blame themselves or their children for an illness, or put off seeing a doctor. Removed with its search, data files, content provider, card widget and tests. The body practices stay in the catalogue's "Body" section; the companion's "notice what the body feels" now opens the article "The body and stress".

### Fixed
- A second launch of the running app (the launcher icon, a reminder) started a second activity; the next tab tap crashed. The activity is now single-top. Found by `tools/android-smoke.sh` on a Release x64 build, which passes after the fix (cold start 2.1 s, 2000 random events).
- Button labels (the "Create" button of the practice catalogue) kept the old language after switching it until a restart. Seen on the emulator: they follow the switch.
- The onboarding still promised "five pillars" with the removed body-and-emotions tab and said everything was in the tab bar; it now names the four tabs and the menu. Seen on the emulator in Russian.
- On the first start after installing, on a slow phone, the practice catalogue could stay "No practices yet" until the app was restarted: the load waited for the encrypted database inside its 10-second timeout, and creating the database took longer. The timeout now covers the load only. Seen once on a cold emulator before the fix; not reproduced after it (a first start cannot be repeated on demand).
- The burger menu closed whatever was open over a tab, so opening it (by an edge swipe on phones with button navigation) from a practice, a test or a chat dropped the unsaved answers. Step pages now have no menu (`PrepareSecondaryPage`, also used by the startup safety check, crisis help and the theory page). Not tried on a device: the emulator uses gesture navigation, where the edge swipe is "back".
- A quote reminder tapped twice no longer stacks two Quotes pages.
- Body check-in: a tap in the middle of the head picked "Face and jaw" (the face now covers only the lower part of the head); a scale left untouched was saved as 5/10 (now "not rated"); a backup's body log is checked on import, and a name from a newer version no longer fails the whole restore; a stored document that cannot be read is set aside instead of being replaced by the next save (for the reading state and the fear ladder too).
- The chat list's mood trend could stay in the neutral colour: a local text colour overrode the improving / worsening triggers.
- The top text of the Body and Journal pages sat right under the bar (they now use the margin of pages with a bar).
- After every restart of the phone the app crashed in the background and the reminders were not set again: the manifest declared the eight reminder receivers under their .NET names, which do not exist in the package (the classes are `crc64…`). The receivers are now declared by their attributes only, each boot receiver with its `BOOT_COMPLETED` filter. Seen on the emulator: after a reboot the system started the boot receiver and nothing crashed.

### Performance
- Measured in Release on an x86_64 emulator (a new `-p:AndroidReleaseRid=android-x64` makes such a build; the arm64 package runs translated there, which made a 1.9 s cold start look like 5.7 s). Cold start: about 1.9 s, no skipped frames.
- Articles: the list is virtualized (a grouped list instead of every card built at once), the section chips are built once instead of on every keystroke, and coming back from an article no longer rebuilds the list when nothing was read or saved. Opening the page: worst frame 250 → 150 ms, slow UI-thread frames 3 → 1.
- Journal: coming back to the tab rebuilt the week strip every time; now only the selection moves when the days are the same. Worst frame 109 → 18 ms.
- Practice list on Android: the card is one native view (`TechniqueCardNativeView` with an Android handler) instead of about 17 native views with a MAUI handler each; it looks the same (colours of the group, icon or picture, text size and bold-text settings) and takes the tap itself, with the same press feedback. Release, 8 fast flings down and 8 up, three runs each, XAML card vs native card: janky frames 3.1–3.7% vs 2.8–3.2%, 95th percentile 21–25 vs 19–21 ms, 99th 27–38 vs 22–27 ms, slow UI-thread frames 1–3 vs 0. The remaining janky frames are drawing on the emulator, not the card. Seen on the emulator in the light theme (the look, a tap opening the practice); not seen: the dark theme, a large font, TalkBack, a practice with its own picture.
- Not changed: the first opening of the profile (about 200 ms, a large page).

### Added

Where (a tap on a front silhouette or a chip, ten places), what it is like (ten sensations) and how strong, then up to three feelings offered as guesses to check (ranked by the bodily maps of emotions, Nummenmaa et al. 2014), all other feelings and "I don't know", two open questions and an optional note. After saving: practices for what was found and "Your patterns" — a place and sensation that came back at least twice, the feeling chosen most often in it and the usual time of day. The texts and weights need a psychologist's review.

- **Trauma work in the chat** ([docs/trauma-module.md](docs/trauma-module.md#trauma-work-in-the-chat)). Not "digging up" memories: leading questions about hidden memories create false ones, and reliving an event without a therapist can flood a person with no one to stop it. Instead, as in cognitive processing therapy without a written account (as effective for PTSD, Resick et al., 2008), the work is on what the event changed.
  - When a hard event is mentioned (abuse, an assault, an accident, war), the companion thanks, says the details are not needed and offers choices; it no longer asks "what happened?".
  - **What it changed in me**: distress 0–10 first; then a theme (blame, safety, trust, control, how I see myself, closeness), the phrase one tells oneself (examples as chips), belief 0–10, two Socratic questions of that theme, a fairer wording, belief again, and a summary ("Blame: 'It was my fault'. Belief was 8, now 3").
  - **What sets it off**: a trigger map in three questions, without the event.
  - **Stabilising first**: distress of 7 or more, or a flashback in the words at any step, stops the work and offers the way back to the present; crisis words open crisis help first; "stop" ends it.
  - What is found (stuck points with belief before and after, triggers) is kept in the chat memory in the encrypted database and goes into the **summary for a specialist**, without details of the event.
  - "Work it through in the chat" in the article "After trauma: where to start" and on day 4 of the trauma course opens a chat straight at these choices.
  - Tested: the reply to a hard event, ordinary words and a denial not read as trauma, a whole stuck-point run noted for the specialist, stabilising at 8 of 10 and on a flashback mid-way, crisis words, the trigger map, the topic chat opening with nothing else, notes kept between chats and in the summary.
  - Seen on the emulator in Russian: the article link opening the chat on the topic, distress, the theme chips, the example phrases, the questions on blame, the summary "8 → 3" with the next choices. Not seen: the trigger map, the reply when a hard event is typed (Cyrillic cannot be typed through adb), the summary export, English, the dark theme.

- **Fear ladder (exposure)**, a programme over weeks rather than one more practice; it sits in the catalogue, first in the "Action" section, so the main page gets no new block. Exposure is the most effective treatment of anxiety (panic, social and specific fears), and it works by learning that a feared outcome does not come, not by waiting for the anxiety to pass (inhibitory learning, Craske et al., 2014). The protocol is in [docs/exposure-engine.md](docs/exposure-engine.md); **it must be reviewed by a clinical psychologist before release.**
  - A safety screening first: thoughts of self-harm lead to the crisis screen; trauma with flashbacks, heavy rituals, dissociation or psychosis, or numbing with substances lead to a kind "better with a specialist"; a body risk (heart, epilepsy, asthma, pregnancy...) leaves out the steps that raise the heart rate on purpose. The screening is asked again after 4 weeks.
  - "My fear": the kind of fear, the worst-case prediction and how sure, the safety behaviours and what is avoided, all by chips (Russian and English, matched one to one, tested), and the anxiety loop the ladder breaks.
  - The ladder: steps picked from examples or written, each rated 0-100 with − and +, always kept in order from easy to hard.
  - A task before (the prediction, how sure, which safety net to give up, the rules) and after (what happened, whether it came true, how sure now, the peak, whether a safety net was used, what was learned). Then the feedback: "belief 7 → 2 of 10", the lesson, and what it means; a prediction that came true gets decatastrophising questions; stopping early is allowed and never called a failure.
  - The rules of moving up are in the domain and tested: a step is mastered after two predictions that did not come true, the last one without a safety net and with the belief down to 3 of 10 or lower; distress deliberately does not decide it. Two overwhelming or stopped tasks in a row suggest an easier step in between. Progress counts predictions tested and how many came true.
  - Words in a report that read as a crisis lead to the crisis screen instead of the next step.
  - Kept in the encrypted database (one JSON document in AppMetadata, source-generated for trimming and AOT), included in backups; restoring an older backup never overwrites a ladder already on the device. Deleting the ladder takes two taps.
  - Seen on the emulator (Pixel, Android 15, English, light and dark): screening, "my fear", building and reordering the ladder, a task before and after, the feedback, the progress after a restart. - `.gitignore` had `Backup*/`, which hid `Infrastructure/Data/Repositories/Backup/BackupRepository.cs` and `Integration.Tests/Backup` from git: a fresh clone did not compile. The pattern is anchored to the repository root and both are now tracked.
- Reminder taps: the notifications' tap intents carry a random per-install secret and MainActivity (exported as the launcher) accepts a reminder action only with it; package name and action alone, which any app can set, no longer count.
- Quotes are unique by text (schema version 14: duplicates removed, unique index, `INSERT OR IGNORE`), so concurrent seedings cannot insert the same quote twice.
- Release builds now keep a local error log (warnings and errors only, `logs/app-errors.log`, capped at 2 MB; crashes are written straight to disk). Before, Release had no log sink and every `LogError` vanished. The data screen has "Share error log" (the file holds technical errors, not entries; nothing is sent automatically).
- Global error handling: raw exception messages (English, about SQL or entities) are no longer shown to the person; cancellations are not reported; an unobserved background task is logged instead of raising a red toast; a terminating crash is written to disk instead of opening a dialog nobody will see.
- Reminders use inexact alarms (`SetAndAllowWhileIdle`) and the `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` permission is gone from the manifest: a daily reminder does not need exact delivery and Google Play reserves that permission for alarm/calendar apps.
- Page-visit statistics (written on every screen view, read by nothing but a distinct-page count) are pruned at startup to 90 days; the latest row of every page is kept.
- Measured and not adopted: a minimum confidence for naming a feeling in the chat (a single clear keyword and an accidental match both score 0.5; see docs/companion-understanding.md).
- Safety: an answer other than "never" to the suicide question of the Beck inventory (item 9) or the PHQ-9 (item 9) is now a risk signal on its own: it is recorded like the risk check (so the startup gate and the dashboard react) and the crisis screen opens right after the result, whatever the total.
- Beck inventory scoring takes the highest ticked statement of each group (the standard rule) instead of summing every ticked statement, so ticking several statements no longer inflates the total and the severity band.
- Chat turns are saved atomically: memory counters, the messages and the chat's state/title go to the database in one transaction (`IChatRepository.SaveTurnAsync`), and turns of one chat run one at a time, so a crash or a double send can no longer leave messages without their state or lose a turn; starting a chat from several taps makes one chat.
- Prayer audio cache: HTTPS only, the content type and the first bytes must be audio (an error page answered with 200 is no longer kept as a track), one shared download per track instead of two writers on one file, a bad file is dropped from the cache when it will not play, the folder is capped at 200 MB (least recently used first), and the player stops waiting for a track after 20 s.
- Startup: a failure while seeding quotes (any exception, or the startup deadline) no longer aborts the start.
- Alice: signing in works (the whole Yandex family over HTTPS is allowed, not only alice.yandex.ru).
- Search in quotes and in the body/psychosomatics catalogue ignores case and "ё" and finds other word forms ("тревога" finds "тревоги").
- Chat: links, e-mail addresses and mixed-case names (iPhone, eBay) are no longer capitalised when stored.
- Feedback form: with no developer address configured it said support would receive the message while only opening the share sheet; it now says so and the button reads "Share". Put an address in `AppSettings.ReviewEmailAddress` to send by e-mail.
- Chat companion: input with nothing to understand is no longer answered as a heartfelt story ("1111" used to get "Thank you for sharing, this seems important to you" and move the dialogue on). Bare digits, key mashing, repeated letters, "???" and "!!!" are recognised; Russian typed on the English layout ("ghbdtn") is read as Russian; one or two empty words and hesitation ("ну", "ммм") get an honest reply; the tension question explains its scale when answered with junk. Noise does not count as a turn and does not change what the companion knows.
- Double taps: a page no longer opens twice. `NavigationCoordinator` used to *queue* a second push of the same destination behind the first (so it opened again 350 ms later); it now drops it while the first is opening and for 600 ms after. Commands that start async work (`AsyncCommand<T>` for mood saves, journal share/select, finishing a paper-list session) ignore a second tap while running, instant steps (onboarding Next/Back, technique wizard steps, test answers, Luscher colour pick, option views) are debounced, and only one dialog or sheet can be on screen at a time. Verified on a Samsung A25: a quick double tap on onboarding "Next" skipped a step before the change and moves one step after it; a double tap on a technique card opens it once.
- Release builds no longer abort at random (SIGABRT, `aot-runtime.c: condition plt_entry not met`, on the .NET Timer / TP Gate threads) a few seconds to a minute after opening Prayers or Quotes: profiled AOT (`AndroidEnableProfiledAot`) is switched off, plain AOT stays. Verified on a Samsung A25 (Android 14): the profiled build crashed three times in 2.5 minutes, the plain-AOT build ran over five minutes under load (tabs, scrolling, prayer playback) without a crash; cold start is unchanged (about 1.6 s) and the package grows by about 9 MB.
- Localization tests that change the static language now run in the `Localization` collection, which removes a source of intermittent failures.
- Backup restore: it now runs in a single transaction (all or nothing), skips rows the database already has (importing a file twice changes nothing), keeps the original dates of session results instead of stamping them with the import moment, and no longer creates extra "completed today" practices that inflated counters and the streak. Format version 2 also carries chat memory, favourite quotes, custom techniques, the risk check, the therapy program and escalations; an unreadable, empty, oversized or newer file is rejected before anything is written. The temporary export file is deleted after sharing.
- Streaks now follow the person's own calendar day instead of the UTC date (a practice at 00:30 in Moscow or at 20:00 in New York no longer lands on the wrong day).
- A "red" risk check routes to the crisis hub only for a week; after that the app asks for a new check instead of opening the crisis hub on every launch, and a months-old "green" no longer hides a bad week in the scorecard.
- The weekly scorecard no longer writes a new escalation event every time a dashboard opens (one per kind per week), and the Amber "hold the week" write that was undone on the next read is gone.
